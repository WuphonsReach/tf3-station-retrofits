# Compatibility with other mods

## In short

- **One known incompatibility: TRM: Industry Emissions**, with "Transport Building Emissions" at "Rebalanced" (its default). Up to now, levels on road and tram stations and harbours did nothing while it was active: each buy was charged and stored, and every rebuild dropped all of that station's levels. Seen in game on 2026-11-07, with TRM loaded after Station Retrofits. Rail stations are not affected.
- **Fixed in the dev version, tested in game in both load orders** (2026-11-08: 162 buys on road stations, harbours and rail stations, every level applied, pollution and noise reductions multiplied with TRM's). A `postRunFn` points TRM's wrapper at a small script of ours, which loads the base station script the way the game does, so our wrapper applies again. It runs after every mod's changes, so the load order does not matter.
- **Workaround** (until the fix is released): set TRM's "Transport Building Emissions" to "Base Game". Changing the load order should not help (read from the code).
- **TRM: Infrastructure Upkeep** works, but it lowers every price and refund: they come from the upkeep the game charges, which that mod scales (TODO.md has the open decision).
- **The other mods that change station values** were read from their code and are expected to stack ([docs/design/findings.md](design/findings.md), "Seen in other mods' code"). None is tested in game with the levels yet (TODO.md).
- **Related, in Depot Retrofits:** the same TRM mod blocked placing new depots when it loaded after Depot Retrofits, fixed there in 1.3-dev (`docs/compatibility.md` in `../tf3-depot-retrofits`).

## Known incompatibilities

### TRM: Industry Emissions

- **The mod.** [TRM: Industry Emissions](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/trm-industry-emissions) (EloudaLightstar, `trm_building_emissions_1`, part of the "Transport Rework Mod" series), read in the version updated 2026-10-27. With its "Transport Building Emissions" setting at "Rebalanced", its `loadConstruction` modifier points the `updateScript` of the base transport buildings at its own wrapper, picking them by `.con` file name, and stores what to call in the capture params (`beOriginalScript`, `beOriginalFn`, `beFactor`, `beName`). Among the stations Station Retrofits levels, it halves the emissions of `modular_terminal` (road and tram stations) and `harbor_modular` (harbours); `modular_station` (rail) has factor 1 and is left alone.
- **How it loads the original.** Its wrapper (`game_mechanics/building_emissions/building_build.script.lua`, `loadOriginal`) runs the `.lua` file named in `beOriginalScript` with `ug_require`, calls the global `data()` that file defines and calls that table's `updateFn` with the capture params. It then scales the result's emission lists, and once more after the result's `terminateConstructionHook`.
- **What went wrong.** Station Retrofits wraps the base station script's `updateFn` in a `loadScript` modifier. The game's own loader (`util.useFn`) goes through that modifier; TRM's `ug_require` does not, so TRM called the unwrapped base script. Seen in game (2026-11-07, TRM after Station Retrofits):
  - A tram station bought catchment 8 to 14 for $1,646,839. After the rebuild its radius was 175 m (217 m before, with level 8 applied from before TRM), and its upkeep TRM's halved base with no level factor. No `applied` line in the log.
  - A road station bought upkeep 1 to 11 and catchment 1 to 11 (22 buys, each charged): radius 175 m and upkeep $202,500/Year through every rebuild. A harbour's upkeep levels 2 to 11 changed nothing.
  - The card then read the unlevelled radius as levelled ("105 -> 175 m") and priced from the unlevelled upkeep, so its prices and refunds fell far below what was paid.
  - The levels themselves are kept in the station's params, so a fixed version applies them at the station's next rebuild.
- **Load order did not matter for the bug**, read from the code: TRM's wrapper loads the base script itself whichever mod runs first, and our wrapper sits on the script, not on the construction. Seen with TRM after us.
- **The fix (dev).** `mod.script.tl`'s `postRunFn` runs after every mod's modifiers. For each base station construction whose update script's capture params name the base script in `beOriginalScript`, it keeps that script as a `util.useFn` reference in `stationRetrofitsOriginal` and points `beOriginalScript` at `station_retrofits_1::/station_original.script.lua`. It writes a fresh `ScriptRef` with the same `fileName`, as TRM: Infrastructure Upkeep edits station constructions in its own `postRunFn`; `updateScript.fileName` stays TRM's, so mods that recognise a station by it see no change. That file is plain Lua and defines `data()`, so TRM can load it; its `updateFn` loads the original with `util.useFn`, which goes through `loadScript` and so comes back wrapped, as without TRM. TRM's wrapper then sits outside ours: our levels apply first, then its halving, and the two multiply. If `util.useFn` fails there, the shim loads the original TRM's way and wraps it with `core.wrapUpdate` itself, so the station still builds with its levels; the failure is logged once.
- **Log lines.** `postRun trmRedirected=N` once per load (2 with TRM at "Rebalanced": road and tram stations share one construction, plus harbours), a `trmRedirect` line per construction, and at logLevel "full" `trmShim loaded ... via=useFn` the first time a Lua state builds each kind. Then the usual `applied kind=street` or `kind=harbour` lines.
- **Seen in game (2026-11-08):** the `postRunFn` edit reaches every Lua state that builds stations (`trmShim loaded … via=useFn` in each, `applied` on every rebuild), and TRM's emissions game script keeps our share of the reduction: a road station's emitter fell to 0.45 of its value at pollution 11 and noise 11, in both orders.
- **Status.** Seen in game; fixed in the dev version and tested in game in both load orders (2026-11-08: 83 buys with TRM before us, 79 with TRM after us, and a road station and a harbour placed). Not tested, and not planned while the mod is unreleased: a save written while the fix was missing, Configure on a levelled station with TRM, TRM at "Base Game". TRM's source is not public, so no bug is filed upstream. Station Retrofits takes no dependency on TRM.

## Overlapping mods

- **TRM: Infrastructure Upkeep** (`trm_upkeep_rebalance_1`) scales the yearly upkeep of every station module (`metadata.maintenanceCost`, 30% to 70%, 50% by default) in its `postRunFn` and leaves build prices alone. Our wrapper scales the upkeep the modules add up to, so the levels' upkeep factor stacks on it. But the level prices come from the station's upkeep (6 times it, as a base module's build price is), so with that mod every price and refund falls by the same factor. A station keeps the upkeep it was built with until its next rebuild: on one built before the mod was switched on, the first removal was priced from the old upkeep (seen 2026-11-07: $159,982 refunded where the level then cost $99,984). Whether to price from something the mod does not scale is open (TODO.md).
